Questions

Before you book

What comes with a hookah rental?

Rentals come with a variety of flavors and coal. They're perfect for small gatherings.

What's the difference between the bar and a rental?

With the mobile hookah bar, we come to your event and cater or vend. With a rental, you get the hookahs, flavors and coal for your own smaller hang.

Where do you serve?

New Jersey, New York, Pennsylvania and Connecticut. We come to you.

Can I earn money when you vend my event?

Yes, you can earn when you book us to vend. Ask us how it works for your event.

Is there an age requirement?

Yes. Hookah service is for guests 21 and older.
