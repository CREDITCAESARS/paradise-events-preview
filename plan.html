What the numbers say

Machines beat everything

A cotton candy machine getting cleaned did 48.4 million views. Two more cotton candy videos did 5.3M and 614K. You have the machines, and nobody around here is filming them.

Small accounts go viral in this niche

A party host with 5,714 followers got 1.2M views. A mobile bar with 2,239 followers got 199K. You don't need followers first.

People love watching the setup

"Mobile bar in 3 steps" did 971K. "Watch me set up" did 930K. Empty space turning into a party is the proof people want before they book.

Short and funny works

A 7-second bartender joke did 437K. A 5-second clip did 199K. Not every post needs editing.

Hookah needs a different play

The best hookah video all year got 5,841 views. TikTok keeps smoking off the For You page. For Puff Paradise, show the fruit carving, the lounge setup and people having a good time, not the smoke.

How to post

  • Put the hook on screen in the first second.
  • Keep most Reels under 30 seconds. The 5 to 15 second ones did as well as minute-long ones.
  • Use the real sound of the machines. The biggest videos had no talking.
  • Carousels are for saves: checklists, FAQs, menus, packs. End each with one ask.
  • Put the town in the caption and location so local parents find it.
  • After two weeks, see which format got the most views and do more of it.
  • Only post guests and kids with the host's okay.
