Questions

Before you book

Do I need every detail figured out before I reach out?

No. Tell us what you're celebrating, your date and what you have in mind. We'll help you pick the services that make sense for your event.

Is the attendant extra on the concession machines?

No. Our concession rentals include the necessary supplies and an attendant at no additional cost.

Is your bartending licensed and insured?

Yes. Paradise Pour mobile bartending is licensed and insured, with custom drink menus, professional bartenders and all supplies included.

Can I book just one thing?

Yes. Book a single service or build the whole event. It's your party.

How far ahead should I book?

As early as you can. Planning ahead gives you time to coordinate rentals and prepare for your guests without feeling rushed.

Do you do hookah?

Yes, through our hookah brand Puff Paradise Hookahs: mobile hookah bar, rentals and fruit hookahs for guests 21+.
